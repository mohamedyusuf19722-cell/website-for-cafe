FAQ

Good to know

Do you offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, free Wi-Fi is available for all guests. Ask at the counter for the password.

Do you have vegetarian and vegan options?

Yes, many of our drinks and bakes are vegetarian, and we can make drinks with plant-based milk on request.

Can I host a birthday or a small party?

Of course. Book a table with a note about your plan and we will help arrange it.

Do you deliver?

Place your order here and we will confirm on WhatsApp whether delivery is available to your area.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, UPI and cards.
